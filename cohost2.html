About

The community voice of Reality Check.


AnIdiot is a co-host of Reality Check and brings a strong community-focused perspective to the show. With a pulse on NFT culture, Web3 communities, and the conversations happening across the space, AnIdiot helps connect project stories back to the people who support them.

Known for his sometimes outrageous sense of humor, quick reactions, and ability to keep the room loose, AnIdiot adds a unique energy to Reality Check. He brings thoughtful questions, real community insight, and the kind of humor that keeps Web3 from taking itself too seriously.

Whether discussing new ecosystems, project updates, market shifts, or creator opportunities, AnIdiot helps keep Reality Check centered on the people, culture, and chaos that make Web3 move.
