About

The market voice of Reality Check.


Kenny Powerz is a co-host of Reality Check and the show's go-to voice for market movement, trending tokens, and active plays across Web3.

Known for reading charts, spotting momentum, and keeping a close eye on what is moving across the market, Kenny helps the community understand where attention is flowing and which opportunities may be worth watching. From trending tokens and meme coins to ecosystem plays and market setups, he brings a trader's perspective to the conversation.

On Reality Check, Kenny focuses on helping the audience cut through the noise, understand the charts, and stay informed on where the plays are forming in real time.
