About

The man behind Reality Check.


Chesus is the founder and host of Reality Check, a daily Web3 show focused on crypto, NFTs, gaming, culture, and the people building the space.

After more than six years in Web3 and hundreds of live conversations, Chesus has interviewed founders, creators, artists, builders, and community leaders from across the industry. His approach is simple: ask real questions, cut through the noise, and give the audience an honest look at what is actually happening in the market.

Reality Check was built to be more than another crypto show. It is a community-first platform where projects can tell their story, founders can connect directly with users, and listeners can discover what is worth paying attention to before the rest of the market catches on.

From NFTs and gaming to emerging chains, creator ecosystems, meme culture, and Web3 infrastructure, Chesus brings experience, curiosity, and an unfiltered perspective to every conversation.
