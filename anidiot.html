About

The one brands call when they want the community to actually feel something.


AnIdiot is a co-host of Reality Check and the person you bring in when you need your community to laugh, respond, and participate. He has a natural ability to make any room feel alive — turning passive audiences into active ones and making brands feel more human in the process.

With a deep pulse on Web3 culture, meme language, creator energy, and what communities actually respond to, AnIdiot helps projects connect with their people in a way that feels real, fun, and impossible to ignore.

Whether it's a live appearance, a segment on Reality Check, or helping a brand sharpen its voice, AnIdiot brings the kind of energy that makes people stick around — and come back.
