The Pieces

How It Works

Collect all four torn pieces as they drop on X. Two on Tuesday, two on Wednesday.
Crack the scroll. It's locked, and the key is split up. Some of it is hidden in the pieces, and some of it gets spoken out loud on Reality Check.
Put the map back together. The pieces won't come to you facing the right way.
Sail the route the scroll gives you to the island it names.
Find that island's character in the Grand Line binder on The CardWall and bring back the slab's cert number.

Claim The Treasure

One shot per crew. You get one entry and we won't tell you if you're right. Double check your route before you send it.

The Rules

  • One entry per person. Extra accounts or repeat entries from the same device get disqualified.
  • To count, an entry needs the right cert number and the first three words of the decoded scroll.
  • The first correct entry wins the treasure slab itself. Runner ups are drawn from everyone else who got it right.
  • Winners have to confirm from the X account they entered with.
  • Keep your answers to yourself. Post your guesses in public and you're handing the treasure to someone else.
  • Entries close Thursday at 12 PM ET. Winners get crowned live on the stream Thursday at 1 PM ET.
